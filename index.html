Our background

We are very pleased to be able to offer deluxe guided tour packages to the people of British Columbia. It is our belief that travel broadens the mind and gives each of us a different perspective on life. Travel is the ultimate educational journey.  We are very proud to be a BC owned and operated business and honored to be able to provide our neighbors with life changing travel experiences.   

Our Tours

All of our tours are carefully planned and expertly laid out to maximize your experience.  Rather than spending hours researching hotels and studying maps and guide books, one call to Mile Zero Tours will get you on your way to an exciting destination with an experienced tour director, with all those details taken care of so you can actually experience the destination.  This is the freedom of guided travel. 

What's included

With Mile Zero Tours you'll stay in first class hotels, eat delicious locally inspired meals, participate in unique cultural experiences, and have the advantages of a dedicated tour director throughout your trip. Our inclusive packages also include the flights and pick up from your front door anywhere in Greater Victoria, Vancouver, or Nanaimo.
